# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in AlphaX a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include respectful
discussion, accepting constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the
project, and showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, public or private
sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information without permission,
and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.
